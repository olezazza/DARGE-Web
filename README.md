# DARGE

**A smarter way to grow.**

DARGE is an agriculture and gardening platform designed to help people plan, manage, and improve their growing projects through technology. The goal is to make gardening more organized, accessible, and data-driven by bringing useful tools and growing knowledge into one place.

## About the Project

Growing plants requires planning, consistency, and an understanding of the conditions that affect them. DARGE aims to simplify this process by providing a digital space where growers can organize their projects, track progress, and learn from their experience.

The long-term vision is to combine gardening tools, community features, and AI-assisted guidance into a single platform.

## Planned Features

- **Growing Projects** — Create and organize individual gardening or growing projects.
- **Task Management** — Keep track of planting schedules, watering, maintenance, and other essential tasks.
- **AI Growing Assistant** — Get personalized guidance, growing recommendations, and help with common gardening questions.
- **Progress Tracking** — Monitor growing activities and maintain a record of progress.
- **Community** — Share experiences, exchange knowledge, and connect with other growers.
- **Gamification** — Encourage consistent growing habits through achievements, experience points, and community challenges.

*These features describe the project's direction and do not necessarily represent the current implementation.*

## Technology Stack

- **Language:** C#
- **Framework:** ASP.NET Core MVC
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Razor views
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (project configuration may vary)
